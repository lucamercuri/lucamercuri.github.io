Privacy Policy

BrightBox Auto - last updated 24 October 2026

BrightBox Auto is a private tool. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channels TheBrightBox TV, Kubbo & Kibbo and The Why Reveal, who runs it on their own computer to manage those channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google account data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these permissions, and uses them only for the owner's own channels:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload) - to publish the owner's finished videos.
  • Manage the YouTube account (youtube, youtube.force-ssl) - to set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, read the channels' comments, and post replies the owner has approved.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) - to show the owner how their videos perform.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner information about their own channels and to carry out the uploads and replies they ask for.
  • Sign-in tokens, statistics and comments are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • Comment text may be shown to an AI assistant on the owner's computer to help draft a reply; nothing is posted without the owner's approval.

BrightBox Auto's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's folder on the owner's computer removes all data it stored.
