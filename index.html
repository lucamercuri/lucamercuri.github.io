BrightBox Auto

A private publishing tool for three YouTube channels run by one creator.

BrightBox Auto is the software the channel owner uses on their own computer to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public: nobody else can sign in to it, and it never acts on anyone else's account.

Channels

  • TheBrightBox TV - long relaxing ambient videos (rain, fireplace, ocean, sleep and focus sounds)
  • Kubbo & Kibbo - wordless animated comedy Shorts
  • The Why Reveal - short animated explainers

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's channels.
  • Reads the channels' own video statistics and comments so the owner can follow how videos perform.
  • Posts comment replies only when the owner approves each one.

How it handles Google account data is described in the privacy policy.
